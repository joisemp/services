The workflow

From report to resolution

Three steps. No confusion about who does what next.

01
Report it

A general user opens a ticket — attaches photos, adds a voice note, and pins the location. Takes under two minutes from any device.

chevron_right
02
Assign & track

A maintainer creates work tasks, assigns team members, and monitors progress. Supervisors escalate or reassign at any point.

chevron_right
03
Resolve & close

Once work is done, a reviewer signs off. Every step is logged — full audit trail, no guesswork for anyone.
